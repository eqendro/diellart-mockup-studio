# Business Workflow

## Current public-preview workflow

1. The visitor selects one supported artwork file.
2. The browser validates and classifies it without uploading it.
3. The customer selects a logo area when guided crop is required.
4. Suitable raster artwork receives local background and margin preparation.
5. The customer selects a detected, black, blue, or green print colour.
6. The prepared one-colour artwork appears on the Pocket Paper Digital Proof.
7. The customer adjusts safe-area-constrained size and placement using
   controls, dragging, or touch gestures.
8. Approval records the current proof only in browser memory.

Replacement or removal clears derived artwork state. Failed automatic
preparation preserves the original or confirmed crop and offers adjustment or
replacement.

## Planned commercial workflow

Quotation submission, customer accounts, persistence, CRM integration, product
catalogue browsing, and order handling are not implemented. They remain future
work after the standalone Pocket Paper public preview.
