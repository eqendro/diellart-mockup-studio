# Business Workflow

**Draft — to be expanded.**

This document will describe the journey from a customer previewing a personalised product through to requesting a quote and entering the existing DiellArt sales process.

## Logo selection workflow

1. The visitor selects one logo through the file picker or drag-and-drop area.
2. The browser validates its type, extension, size, and readability.
3. If validation fails, the visitor receives an actionable message and can try again.
4. If validation succeeds, the browser displays the original logo with basic file metadata.
5. The visitor may replace or remove the logo at any time.

At this stage the logo remains temporary and browser-local. It is not uploaded, transformed, saved, scored for print readiness, applied to a product, or included in a quotation. Future logo-engine and mockup-engine steps will extend the workflow after their requirements are approved.
